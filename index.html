Dennis Kenney, Executive Communication Coach and Speaker

Ivy Level Speaking offers executive communication coaching, keynotes, and brand strategy from Dennis Kenney. Dennis is a professor of Strategic Communication at NYU Stern, a coach at Harvard Business School and Columbia Business School, a TEDx speaker, and the bestselling author of The Unexpected Stage. He works with C suite leaders and Fortune 500 teams in person in New York and Boston and virtually worldwide.

Services include executive presence coaching, interview and job talk preparation, media training, personal brand, academic presentations, corporate workshops, keynote speaking, and speech, debate, and admissions interview coaching for middle school and high school students.
